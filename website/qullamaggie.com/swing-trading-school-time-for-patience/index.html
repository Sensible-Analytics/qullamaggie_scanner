Overview & Key Thesis

This Swing Trading School session (August 12, 2020) focuses on patience as a trading virtue. Kristjan emphasizes that during choppy, sideways market periods, the best action is often no action. He reviews numerous stock setups, most of which he says need "a few more days" or "next week" before becoming tradeable — reinforcing the idea that forcing trades in a suboptimal environment is destructive.

Core Message: "Every single one of you in chat right now, if you really put in the work and understand the nuances of the setup... you're guaranteed you're gonna make millions, tens of millions. GUARANTEED." But most won't because they won't do the work, and they won't sit out the bad times.

Market Awareness & Timing

When to Trade Breakouts (NASDAQ 10/20 SMA Rule):

  • Sideways, choppy: Break even = good job. Don't force it.
